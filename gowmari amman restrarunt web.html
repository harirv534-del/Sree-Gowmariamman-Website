About Us

Three counters, one family kitchen

Sree Gowmariamman has been Theni's roadside stop for a proper meal, a fresh cake and a hot cup of coffee — all from the same kitchen. We keep our recipes traditional, our bakery fresh daily, and our doors open long hours so there's always a reason to pull in.

From family lunches to birthday cake pickups to a quick coffee before the bus, we've built the space to make all three easy — with parking, seating and service to match.
